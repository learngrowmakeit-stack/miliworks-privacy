Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Developer: MiliWorks
Contact: learn.grow.makeit@gmail.com

MyOrganizer helps you organize folders, file links, tasks and appointments. The app does not require a MyOrganizer account. The current version does not include advertising, analytics or developer-operated servers, and does not send your organizer contents to the developer.

Information stored on your device

MyOrganizer stores folder names, links to files you select, task titles, favorite and completion status, appointment dates and times, reminder choices, and theme settings. Image previews are loaded from selected files and may be cached locally. Files are opened through compatible apps you choose; those apps and your file providers have their own privacy practices.

If you add the optional home-screen widget, open task titles and the next appointment are displayed on your phone’s launcher. Anyone who can view that home screen may see them. You can remove the widget without deleting organizer data.

Permissions

The Android document picker grants access to individual files you select; MyOrganizer does not request broad access to all files on your device. Notification permission allows appointment reminders. The optional alarms permission permits precise reminder timing. The boot permission lets the app reschedule reminders after restarting the phone.

Exported backups

You can export a JSON backup to a location you choose. It contains organizer data and file links, not copies of the original documents and photos. Treat it as personal information. If you choose a cloud storage provider as the destination, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. You can restore this file to merge organizer data with the data on your device. File access may need to be granted again after reinstalling or changing devices.

Android backup and device transfer

The current app enables Android's system backup mechanism. Depending on your device and system settings, Android may back up or transfer app data through services associated with your system account. This is separate from manual JSON exports. MyOrganizer's developer does not receive or operate those backups.

Retention and deletion

Organizer data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or remove its local storage. Uninstalling can remove local organizer data; Android may restore a system backup on later installation. Removing a file link from MyOrganizer does not delete the original file. Exported backups remain in the locations you selected until you delete them there. Removing an appointment cancels its scheduled reminder. Deleted entries may remain briefly in memory while the Undo notice is available.

Optional feedback

If you choose Send feedback, MyOrganizer opens your email app with a draft containing the app version, Android version and device manufacturer/model. You can review and edit it before sending. Organizer entries and files are not attached automatically. If you send the email, MiliWorks receives your message and sender email address and uses them to respond and diagnose problems. Sending is optional and delivery is handled by your chosen email provider. If no email app is available, you can choose to copy the template instead.

Contact and changes

Questions about privacy: learn.grow.makeit@gmail.com. Update this policy if the app's data handling or services change.
